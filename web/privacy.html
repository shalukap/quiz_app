Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how QuizMaster ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile or web application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not access or use the App.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a personalized question bank and MCQ quiz experience, we collect specific information from and about you.

A. Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Registration Data: When you sign up using your email and password, we collect your email address, chosen username, and securely hashed passwords (managed entirely by Firebase Authentication).
  • Profile Personalization: If you choose to customize your profile, we collect your custom profile photo (uploaded from your device via the Image Picker to Firebase Storage) and grade selection (1–13) or subject preferences.

B. Information Collected via Third-Party Authentication

  • Google Sign-In: If you sign in using Google, we retrieve standard profile information from Google, including your display name (used to pre-populate your username), email address, Google profile photo URL, and a unique Google identifier (UID).

C. Application Usage & Performance Data

  • Quiz Activity & Statistics: To calculate your progress and maintain leaderboard stats, we save: number of quizzes taken, your score, overall accuracy percentage, timestamps of each quiz attempt, your specific quiz responses, and accumulated points.

D. Automatically Collected Technical Information

Since the App is built using Google Firebase services, certain technical data may be automatically logged, including IP address, device brand, model, operating system, standard network request details, and crash logs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to operate, maintain, and improve QuizMaster, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and personalize the quiz application (e.g., custom grade/subject questions).
  • Manage your user account, authenticate your identity, and secure your access.
  • Track progress, record quiz scores, calculate accuracy statistics, and display your position on the Leaderboard.
  • Display your custom profile name and avatar.
  • Provide customer support, including password reset emails and service notifications.

3. Storage and Security of Data

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard backend cloud infrastructure:

  • Firebase Authentication: Handles password security and user credentials securely. We do not store or see your raw passwords.
  • Cloud Firestore: Houses user profiles (points, accuracy, quizzes taken), subject questions, and quiz result records.
  • Firebase Storage: Securely hosts uploaded user profile images.

We implement standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal data. However, please be aware that no storage or transmission method over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We share your information only in the following scenarios:

  • Public Leaderboards: Other registered users of QuizMaster can see your username, profile photo, accumulated points, and accuracy stats on the App's Leaderboard screen.
  • Service Providers: We share data with Google Firebase, which hosts, secures, and maintains our infrastructure.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or government regulation.

5. Third-Party Services Used

Our app integrates with third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase: Used for backend databases and account authentication. Google Privacy & Terms
  • Google Sign-In: Used to authenticate your account. Google Identity Platform Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts: Delivers beautiful typography for the application. Google Fonts FAQ

6. Your Rights and Data Control

We believe in giving you full control over your personal data:

  • Update Profile Info: You can update your profile photo, grade level, and subject choices directly within the profile settings in the App.
  • Data Deletion Requests: You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated quiz history. To request data deletion, please contact us at the support email provided below.

7. Children's Privacy

QuizMaster allows selection of school grades (Grades 1–13). If you are under the age of 13, please use this App only under the supervision of a parent, legal guardian, or school educator. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental or guardian consent. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information without consent, we will take immediate steps to delete such data from our servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any updates.
