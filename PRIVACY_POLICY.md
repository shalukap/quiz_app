# Privacy Policy for QuizMaster

**Last Updated: June 2, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how QuizMaster ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile or web application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide a personalized question bank and MCQ quiz experience, we collect specific information from and about you.

### A. Personal Information You Provide
*   **Account Registration Data:** When you sign up using your email and password, we collect your email address, chosen username, and securely hashed passwords (managed by Firebase Authentication).
*   **Profile Personalization:** If you choose to personalize your profile, we may collect:
    *   Custom profile photos (uploaded from your device via the Image Picker to Firebase Storage).
    *   Grade selection (1–13) and subject preferences (e.g., Mathematics, Science) to customize your quiz content.

### B. Information Collected via Third-Party Authentication
*   **Google Sign-In:** If you sign in using Google, we retrieve standard profile information from Google, including:
    *   Your display name (used to pre-populate your username).
    *   Your email address.
    *   Your Google profile photo URL (used as your avatar in the app).
    *   A unique Google identifier (UID).

### C. Application Usage & Performance Data
*   **Quiz Activity & Statistics:** To calculate your progress and maintain leaderboard data, we save:
    *   Number of quizzes taken.
    *   Your score and overall accuracy percentage.
    *   The timestamp of each quiz attempt.
    *   Your specific answers to quiz questions (to generate review screens).
    *   Accumulated points (used for ranking on the Leaderboard).

### D. Automatically Collected Device & Technical Information
Since the App is built using Firebase services, certain technical data may be automatically collected, including:
*   Your IP address.
*   Device brand, model, operating system, and unique device identifiers.
*   Standard network request details (including Google Fonts requests).
*   Crash logs and performance analytics.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and improve QuizMaster, including:
*   **Providing and Personalizing the App:** Customizing quiz questions based on your selected grade, subject, and medium of instruction.
*   **Managing Your Account:** Authenticating your identity during login and securing your access.
*   **Tracking and Rewarding Progress:** Saving your quiz history, calculating accuracy stats, and displaying scores on public leaderboards.
*   **Profile Management:** Displaying your custom avatar and username.
*   **Customer Support & Communication:** Responding to password reset requests and providing user support.

---

## 3. Storage and Security of Data

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard backend cloud infrastructure:
*   **Firebase Authentication:** Handles password security and user credentials securely. We do not store or see your raw password.
*   **Cloud Firestore:** Houses user statistics (points, accuracy, quizzes taken), subject questions, and quiz result records.
*   **Firebase Storage:** Securely hosts uploaded user profile images.

We implement standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal data. However, please be aware that no storage or transmission method over the internet is 100% secure.

---

## 4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We share your information only in the following scenarios:
*   **Public Leaderboards:** Other registered users of QuizMaster can see your username, profile photo, accumulated points, and accuracy on the App's Leaderboard screen.
*   **Service Providers:** We share data with third-party service providers (specifically Google Firebase) who host, secure, and maintain the App's services.
*   **Legal Compliance:** We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or government regulation.
